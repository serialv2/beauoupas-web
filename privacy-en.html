2. Data we collect

2.1 Data you provide directly

  • Account credentials: username, email address, password (encrypted, never stored in plain text)
  • Profile: profile picture (optional), date of birth, gender, bio
  • Created content: projects (photos + title + description), voting series, quizzes, chat messages, reports
  • Preferences: language, notification settings, dating preference

2.2 Data collected automatically

  • Technical data: phone model, Android version, app version, unique device identifier
  • Usage data: votes, viewed projects, joined series, last login
  • Location: city and country only (only if you join a "TV mode" series and accept location). Precise geolocation is never stored.
  • Advertising identifiers: Google Advertising ID for serving AdMob ads (see section 4)

2.3 Data we do NOT collect

  • Your phone number (unless you provide it voluntarily in your profile — optional)
  • Your contacts
  • Your SMS, emails, or photos that aren't explicitly uploaded
  • Your precise GPS location

3. How we use your data

  • Run the service: create your account, display your projects to others, enable voting, chat, series
  • Secure the service: detect fraud, multiple accounts, moderate reported content
  • Send transactional emails: signup confirmation, password reset, important notifications
  • Send push notifications: new match, new invitation, new message (you can disable in Settings → Notifications)
  • Improve the application: aggregated and anonymous usage statistics
  • Display ads: AdMob banners and videos (which can earn you credits if you watch them)

GDPR legal basis: contract performance (article 6.1.b) for service operation, and consent (article 6.1.a) for push notifications and personalized advertising.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: know what data we have about you
  • Right to rectification: modify inaccurate data (most are editable directly in the app under Settings → Profile)
  • Right to erasure: request deletion of your account and all your data. You can do it directly from the app: Profile → Danger zone → Delete my account
  • Right to portability: receive your data in a readable format
  • Right to object: refuse certain processing (push notifications, personalized advertising)
  • Right to restrict: ask to freeze data processing
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, email us at steenkiste.ai@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days maximum.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can file a complaint with the CNIL (French supervisory authority): https://www.cnil.fr/en/plaintes, or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence within the EU.

7. Cookies and technical identifiers

Mobile application

BeauOuPas uses the following technical identifiers stored locally on your phone:

  • Supabase session tokens (to keep you logged in)
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging token (to receive push notifications)
  • Google Advertising ID — you can reset or block it in Android settings: Settings → Privacy → Ads
  • Local preferences (language, session state)

Website

Our website (beauoupas.fr and serialv2.github.io/beauoupas-web) does not use tracking cookies. Public pages (this policy, ToS, etc.) are entirely static.

8. Minor users

BeauOuPas is restricted to users aged 18 and over. Registration of minors is prohibited. If we discover that an account belongs to a minor, it will be immediately deleted.

If you are a parent and believe your child has created an account without authorization, contact us at steenkiste.ai@gmail.com and we will delete the account within 48 hours.

9. Data security

  • Passwords: encrypted with bcrypt, never stored in plain text
  • Database connection: encrypted with TLS
  • App ↔ server communication: HTTPS only
  • Authentication: managed by Supabase Auth (industry-standard JWT)
  • Moderation: integrated reporting system to block abusive behaviors

No system is invulnerable. In case of a data breach, we will inform you within 72 hours in accordance with GDPR.
